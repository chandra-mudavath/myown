About UrTax

Simplifying US tax filing with expertise, accuracy, and care

UrTax Consulting, Inc. (UR Tax) is a full-service tax consulting firm dedicated to helping individuals and businesses navigate the complexities of US taxation.

Since 2003, we have provided professional tax preparation, tax planning, tax compliance, accounting, bookkeeping, payroll, and business advisory services to individuals, entrepreneurs, and small to medium-sized businesses.

Our goal is simple: make US tax filing easier, more accurate, and less stressful for our clients.

Who we are

Your trusted US tax & accounting professionals

Tax laws can be complex and constantly changing. At UrTax, we combine professional tax expertise, structured processes, and personalized service to help our clients stay compliant and make informed financial decisions.

We work with our clients throughout the process, not just at filing time.

Our approach

From information collection to tax filing

We believe tax preparation should be a clear and organized process. Our team works through each filing systematically, from collecting client information and supporting documents to preparing, reviewing, filing, and tracking the return.
