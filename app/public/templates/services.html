ITIN PREPARATION

Official IRS Individual Taxpayer Identification Number application and renewal support.

An ITIN is issued by the IRS to individuals who are required to have a US taxpayer identification number for tax filing, treaty benefits, or banking, but do not have and are not eligible for a Social Security Number (SSN).

UrTax guides you through Form W-7 preparation, assists with certified document requirements, and ensures your application is properly assembled and attached to your federal tax return.

  • ITIN renewal for expired numbers
  • Foreign status and identity document verification
  • Spouse and dependent ITIN applications

FBAR & FATCA FILING

Comprehensive foreign financial asset reporting and international compliance.

US citizens, permanent residents, and resident aliens with aggregate foreign bank accounts exceeding $10,000 at any time during the calendar year must file an annual FBAR report with FinCEN.

UrTax helps compile your foreign bank accounts, convert maximum balances using official US Treasury exchange rates, and file FinCEN 114 and IRS Form 8938 accurately.

  • FinCEN Form 114 (FBAR) electronic filing
  • IRS Form 8938 (FATCA Statement of Specified Foreign Assets)
  • Foreign bank, investment, and brokerage account reporting
  • Foreign mutual funds, pensions, and insurance policy review
