Who is a referrer?

A referrer is an existing {{ app_name }} customer who shares their referral code with others.

Who is a referee?

A referee is a new customer who signs up with {{ app_name }} using a referrer's code.

Who is eligible to refer?

Any registered {{ app_name }} customer can refer others. The referee must be new to {{ app_name }} and must use your referral code when signing up.

How much can I earn?

You earn $10 for each referred individual tax return and $20 for each referred business filing or incorporation that is successfully completed.

When will I receive my reward?

Rewards are credited to your account wallet shortly after your referee's tax return has been completed and filed.

Is there a minimum amount before I can redeem?

No. There is no minimum earning threshold before you can redeem your rewards.

Is there a limit on how many people I can refer?

No. You can refer as many friends, family members, and businesses as you like.

What if my referee forgets to use my code?

A referral can only be credited when the referral code is used at sign-up. Contact our support team as soon as possible if a code was missed and we will review it.
