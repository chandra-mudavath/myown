Questions, answered

Everything you need to know

Who is {{ app_name }} for?

{{ app_name }} is designed for tax preparers, staff members, and administrators who manage client returns and compliance work.

Can my staff work from the same case information?

Yes. The workspace is designed to give authorized team members a shared view of documents, status, notes, and next actions.

Does it support both individual and business returns?

The workflow can organize individual and business tax cases with their supporting documents and filing milestones.

Can clients track the progress of their return?

Yes. Clients sign in to their own portal to see where their filing stands, upload requested documents, and receive notifications when something needs their attention.

What types of documents can be uploaded?

You can upload PDFs, images (PNG and JPG), Word and Excel files, CSV, and plain text files, up to 10 MB each.

What happens when a document is replaced?

Documents are versioned, so uploading a new copy keeps the earlier version in the case history instead of overwriting it.

How are staff responsibilities organized?

Staff are assigned roles such as initiator, preparer, reviewer, and manager, so each case moves through clear hand-offs from intake to final review.

How is my data kept secure?

Passwords are stored using strong one-way hashing, email addresses are verified, sessions expire automatically, and repeated failed sign-in attempts are tracked.

What if I forget my password?

Use the "Forgot password" link on the sign-in page and we will email you a secure link to set a new one.

How do I get started?

Create an account to open your workspace, then begin by setting up your first client profile.
